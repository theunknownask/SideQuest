# ⚔️ SideQuest

> *Turn your unwanted chores into an epic RPG adventure! Do chores you dislike to earn XP and coins, then spend them on awesome themes and custom rewards.*

---

## 🌟 Features

* **🛡️ Chore Quest Generator:** Summon custom quests for everyday tasks (like washing dishes or cleaning your room).
* **🚫 Strict Profanity Filter:** Automatically rejects spam or inappropriate words to keep things clean.
* **⏱️ Hold-to-Claim Mechanics:** Hold down the claim button to complete quests and watch them disappear.
* **🎨 Theme Shop:** Unlock and switch between legendary themes:
  * ⚪ **Real Madrid** (*Hala Madrid*)
  * 🔵🔴 **Barcelona** (*Més que un club*)
  * 🟢⚫ **My Hero Academia** (*Plus Ultra*)
  * 🟡🔴 **One Piece** (*Find the One Piece*)
* **🌍 Multi-Language Support:** Fully reactive translation and RTL layout support for **English**, **Arabic**, **Persian (Farsi)**, and **Spanish**.
* **🖼️ Custom Flag Uploads:** Easily drag and drop or upload your own flag images for each language!
* **🎁 Custom Rewards Shop:** Spend your hard-earned coins on custom real-life treats.
